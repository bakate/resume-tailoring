# Model structured resumes with field provenance

The Source Profile and Tailored Resume use structured sections and entries rather than a flat collection of prose claims. Every reformulable professional field retains links to the Candidate Facts that support it, allowing the application to reconstruct a complete resume while rejecting unsupported content before preview or export.

## Consequences

Identity and contact values remain local and do not require professional provenance. Experiences preserve employer, role, dates, context, and achievements; skills, education, languages, projects, and certifications remain distinct sections. ADR-0001 remains applicable to every generated professional statement and is extended from claim-level validation to field-level provenance.
